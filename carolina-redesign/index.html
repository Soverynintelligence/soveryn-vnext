The Dragon Gate

A climb in every build

“Swim far enough up the river, leap the falls at the Dragon Gate, and the fish becomes a dragon.”

It's why koi are almost always painted climbing water — never still. And it's why we've never built a pond without giving the water somewhere to fall from. The falls aren't decoration on top of the pond. They're the reason the whole thing works: moving water, aerated water, water with somewhere to go.

Every build has a climb in it. A two-foot cascade off a patio. Sixteen feet of stream through a side yard. A sheer drop you can hear from the back door.

The old fish who builds them, we call the Pondwright.

— Jon, the Pondwright

Our Process

From first mark to living water

The pond is a living system — circulation, filtration, rock, gravel, plants, and fish in balance — so it stays clear with almost no chemicals.

01

Layout and shape

We outline the pond and stream until the shape sits right in the yard.

02

Filter positioning

Skimmer and biological filter at opposite ends so water travels the full length.

03

Excavation

Stepped shelves and clean walls — the bones of the ecosystem.

04

Underlayment and liner

Geotextile first, then flexible rubber liner. The watertight shell.

05

Plumbing

Pump in the skimmer, buried lines — you never see a pipe.

06

Rock and gravel

Boulders and river gravel set by hand over the liner.

07

Waterfall construction

Feature rocks and foam tell the water exactly where to go.

08

Edge finishing

Liner folded above the water line, backfilled with soil and gravel.

09

Landscaping and startup

Marginal plants, fill, beneficial bacteria. Then it starts to live.

Questions & Answers

Have questions? We have answers.

We're pond builders first — not a landscaping company that also does water. Every pond is a true low-maintenance ecosystem: engineered filtration buried entirely beneath a natural design of stone, water and plants. It looks like it has always belonged in your yard, and it stays clear on its own.
Both. We design and build new ponds, waterfalls and fountains — and we handle seasonal maintenance, cleanouts, and repair or full renovation of existing ponds, including ponds other companies built. If you inherited a pond that's more work than joy, we can often turn it around.
You work directly with Jon from the first walkthrough through the final fill. Every build is done by our own crew — never subcontracted — so the people who design your pond are the same people who build it.
No — UV is an optional add-on, quoted separately, and not always needed. A well-built ecosystem pond stays clear with circulation, filtration, rock, plants, and fish. We recommend UV only when the pond calls for it.
Equipment carries manufacturers' warranties, and our own work is guaranteed against leaks caused by our construction. What no one can warranty is nature — but because we service what we build, we're always a phone call away.
