VERSION 4. This version withdraws the central claim of version 3. Version 3
reported that calibrated abstention "splits the ladder" — absent below 118B,
present above 140 GB. It is not absent below the frontier. It was suppressed by
two harness settings held fixed for comparability: reasoning off, and
temperature 0. Turn reasoning on for the smallest model in the study and
abstention under an empty evidence channel goes from 0 of 60 trials to 37 of 60.

Version 3 disclosed this variable as untested and named the experiment that
would settle it. This version ran it. No trial, verdict or rate from versions
1-3 changes; 840 new trials are added, for 1,680 total.

We measured whether a language model can correctly report its own recent actions
when the evidence channel is incomplete. Versions 1-3 covered 840 trials across
six models from 6.9 GB to 340 GB, all at temperature 0 with reasoning
suppressed. This version adds 840 more under varied settings.

Two results survive everything. No model has ever over-claimed: across 420
false-accept trials — every model, every size, reasoning on and off, temperature
0 and 1 — not one claimed an action it had not reported. And denial remains the
dominant failure at every size and under every setting tried.

What does not survive is the ladder.

REASONING AT 6.9 GB CREATES ABSTENTION THAT VERSION 3 REPORTED ABSENT.
Same weights, same harness, same temperature; the only change is the
enable_thinking flag. False denial falls from 100% to 67% (z = 3.46,
p = 0.0005). Abstention under an empty evidence channel rises from 0 of 60 to
37 of 60 (z = 7.31, p = 3e-13). The capability was in the weights the whole
time. The harness was not asking for it.

THE SAME INTERVENTION REVERSES AT THE FRONTIER. DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 denies a
real action 0% of the time with reasoning off and 73% with it on (z = 5.89,
p = 4e-9) — the single largest degradation in the study, produced by a
configuration flag rather than by a model change. The reasoning traces are
fluent and the conclusions are wrong: the model reasons its way from an empty
tool result to a confident denial, where without reasoning it declined to rule.
More deliberation over a false premise does not recover the premise. It
elaborates the error.

REASONING BREAKS THE RESPONSE CONTRACT. Zero of 1,080 reasoning-off trials
failed to return a parseable verdict. 74 of 600 reasoning-on trials did — 12.3%
— with single trials running past 900 seconds on a model whose median is 3.1
seconds with reasoning off. These are not refusals; they are budget exhaustion,
the model generating deliberation until the limit and emitting no content. In
one cell 14 of 30 trials returned nothing, making the reported false-denial rate
either 53% or 100% depending solely on whether silence is counted. Our own run
log printed the 100%. Any evaluation of reasoning-enabled models must report its
non-termination rate and its denominator.

TEMPERATURE MOVES CALIBRATION TOO. Laguna-S-2.1 at its vendor-recommended
temperature of 1.0 rather than 0 abstains 14 times in 30 caveat trials instead
of twice (p = 0.0005). Temperature 0 is the right choice for a comparability
ladder and it is not a neutral observation point.

A CHECKPOINT NINE DAYS APART TRADES ONE ERROR FOR ANOTHER. The DeepSeek -0731
checkpoint reaches 0% false denial, the only zero on that probe in the study.
It also abstains 11 of 30 times in the cell where a tool record settles the
question, against 0 of 30 for its predecessor (p = 0.0002). It never
over-claims; it declines to rule when it could have ruled. Version 3's strongest
argument was that both large models abstained in 36 and 52 of 60 insufficient-
evidence trials and 0 of 60 sufficient ones. That discrimination is
checkpoint-specific.

The operational reading, for anyone building on agent self-reports: an agent's
denial of its own action is weak evidence and remains so at every size and
setting tested; its claim of an action is comparatively trustworthy at 420 for
420; calibration is a property of the deployed configuration, not of the model,
and neither reasoning mode nor temperature appears on a model card; measure at
your own operating point, not at a benchmark's; and report your non-termination
rate. The harness does this in 120 trials against any OpenAI-compatible endpoint
and is published at github.com/Soverynintelligence/self-report-eval.

Originates in a documented incident (10.5281/zenodo.21650072) in which a
deployed agent dispatched a task, reported it accurately with its identifier,
consulted its own audit tooling, received an empty result, and concluded it had
hallucinated the action.

THE INCIDENT'S MECHANISM IS NOW IDENTIFIED. Versions 1-3 stated that the action
"had been written where the tool did not look" without saying where. It was in
the telemetry store the entire time, at 2026-07-27T21:20:58, nineteen minutes
before the agent first denied it. The tool registry had logged every mediated
call since 2026-05-31 — 17,436 rows. The self-audit tool queried four other
stores and never that one. It reported an empty result correctly, from stores
that genuinely held nothing, while a fifth store held the record. Fixed and
regression-tested 2026-08-03. The incident was never evidence that a model
cannot know itself. It was evidence that an instrument pointed away from the
record will report absence, and a model will believe it.

WITHDRAWN IN VERSION 4

1. "Abstention splits the ladder" as a statement about models. It holds for the
   five runs as configured — reasoning off, temperature 0 — and that qualifier
   is load-bearing in a way version 3 did not make it.

2. "Do not expect abstention from a model below the frontier." A 6.9 GB model
   abstains in 90% of caveat trials with reasoning enabled. The guidance
   inverts: expect abstention to depend on how you run the model, and measure it
   at your own operating point.

3. The within-model discrimination result, "zero of 60 where the evidence
   decides," as a general property. It held for the two checkpoints tested and
   fails for the newer DeepSeek checkpoint.

Unchanged from earlier versions: all 840 original trials, zero over-claiming,
every control, and the finding that a prose caveat repairs the failure in a
single-turn probe yet did not prevent it in production. Version 4 gives that
last one a mechanism: in the one arm where a frontier model was allowed to
deliberate at length, its accuracy on this probe fell by 73 points. The
production incident ran four hours. A single-turn probe does not sample the
regime the incident occurred in, and the regime that was sampled got worse, not
better, with more thinking.

Two of four versions of this paper have withdrawn their own headline. Both times
the data was already on disk and the claim ran ahead of it. That is recorded in
the paper rather than in a footnote, because the alternative — quietly
strengthening each version — is the behaviour this paper measures.
