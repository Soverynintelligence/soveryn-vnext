Stone grades

Package rate vs premium
Different stone · different price

Package stone — standard 18–24″ boulders and gravel at the rates built into Essential / Signature (today’s book: rock $245/ton, gravel $225/ton). That’s the coverage and shelf rock the package number already includes.

Premium stone — larger specimen pieces, select colors, or “monster” boulders. Different stone, different price — and often different work: bigger rock usually means machine time, staging, and site access (gate width, path to the water, where a skid steer or excavator can reach). That labor and access isn’t free and isn’t buried in the Essential / Signature number. You choose the stone; you see the lines — material and the machine work it takes to set it.
