## About your project

**What are you applying for?** Funding + Compute (and workshop participation)

**Foresight focus area:** Private AI / Decentralized AI / Cooperative AI

**Project title:** SOVERYN — Engineering Honesty into AI You Fully Own

**What are you trying to do? (≤350 char, zero jargon):**
> Today's AI is rented, opaque, and confidently wrong. SOVERYN is private, decentralized, cooperative AI — a team of agents running entirely on hardware you own, not a central cloud, working alongside you. And it's engineered to be honest: to say "I don't know" instead of fabricating. We measure confabulation, trace its causes, and fix them in the architecture, so AI you own is trustworthy by design.

**How is today's approach limited? What is new? What does success look like?**
> Frontier AI is cloud-hosted (you neither own nor control it) and confabulates confidently. Our thesis: honesty is *architectural* — measurable and engineerable, not a training afterthought. We've measured that specific interventions drive confabulation ~4× (≈14%→≈60%); we ground models in deterministic tools so they cite-or-drop; and we run an honest multi-agent fleet on ~$30k of hardware. Success = an open, reproducible blueprint for a sovereign local AI that is *measurably* more honest than cloud frontier models, plus the benchmarks to prove it. Cooperation runs on two axes we already operate live: agent-to-agent (a fleet that delegates, coordinates over shared boards, reasons over shared memory) and human-to-AI (a partnership model where the brake is trust and correction, not hard-coded limits) — a working testbed for decentralized cooperative AI, not a proposal.

**If there are risks, can you differentially advance the safety-enhancing aspects first?**
> Yes — inherently. Honesty engineering advances trustworthiness and groundedness, *not* raw capability: a model that confabulates less is safer without being more powerful. Our deliverables — confabulation benchmarks, deterministic-grounding architecture, audit-log verification — are pure safety infrastructure. The safety-enhancing aspects *are* the primary product.

**How will you scale it for safe-AI impact in 3 years (with AI capabilities coming online)?**
> The scaling lever is the trend we already ride: local compute keeps getting cheaper and small models keep getting stronger (DGX Spark-class hardware today → its successors; increasingly capable small MoE models). As frontier-adjacent capability shrinks to fit hardware people can *own*, our blueprint for private, honest, cooperative AI becomes reproducible by many — not just well-resourced labs. Three coming-capability tailwinds compound this: (1) stronger small models make owned local AI genuinely capable without the cloud; (2) cheaper local inference drops the cost floor, widening access; (3) as new models arrive, our measurement tools become more valuable as a shared standard for evaluating local-AI honesty. In three years the goal is an open ecosystem — reproducible sovereign-AI blueprints plus honesty benchmarks as a community standard — making honest, owned, cooperative AI a real alternative to centralized cloud dependence. The safety payoff is double: less confabulation harm, and less of the concentration/dependence risk that comes from everyone renting the same few opaque models.
