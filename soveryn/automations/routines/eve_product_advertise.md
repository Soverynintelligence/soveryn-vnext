## How

1. Pick one brand lane (SOVERYN / ActTruth / CWG).
2. **CWG voice:** oasis, serenity, ecosystems, wildlife, outdoor beauty — never catalog/MAP pricing.
3. Canva with a real image under `data/media/` (pond photos for CWG).
4. `compose_post` (Signal drop) — ready-to-paste caption.
5. No invented stats. One brand, one job.

## Verify

- A draft lands on Signal (via compose_post) or an honest gap line in CC inbox.
- No fabricated metrics.
